# Forward review of the updated editing guidance

October 25, 2026. Six new, constructed cases were reviewed after the instruction update.
All source passages and revisions were generated by the same assistant, so this is a
manual functional review, not an independent human preference study. These passages
were not used to train the detector or select its threshold, and were not detector-scored.
Now that they are published, treat them as regression examples, not future unseen tests.

## 1. Scheduling email: connect the supplied reason

Source: I can photograph the event on Saturday. I need access at 8 a.m. There is a reason
for this. Setting up the lights takes an hour, and guests arrive at 9 a.m.

Revision: I can photograph the event on Saturday, but I need access at 8 a.m. because
setting up the lights takes an hour and guests arrive at 9 a.m.

Review: The connection replaces empty setup while retaining Saturday, both times and
the supplied setup duration. No new logistical requirement was added.

## 2. Product description: retain the dependency

Source: Search works in airplane mode. The app searches notes downloaded to the device.
This is useful. New notes still need an internet connection for their first download.

Revision: Search works in airplane mode because the app searches notes downloaded to
the device, although new notes still need an internet connection for their first download.

Review: The revision connects the offline behavior to the supplied mechanism and retains
the first-download qualification. It does not imply that synchronization also works offline.

## 3. Observational result: preserve the limit on inference

Source: Participants who kept a reading log finished more chapters. We did not assign
people to keep a log. The difference could reflect their existing motivation. We cannot
conclude that keeping the log caused the difference.

Revision: Participants who kept a reading log finished more chapters, but we cannot
conclude that the log caused the difference because participants chose whether to keep
one and the result could reflect their existing motivation.

Review: The result remains observational. The explanation supports the limit on the
causal claim, not a new claim that motivation actually caused the result. The choice
formulation assumes voluntary participation from the source's nonassignment context;
for a strict evidence-preserving edit, retain "we did not assign people to keep one"
instead, since nonassignment alone does not establish how the groups formed.

Corrected final revision: Participants who kept a reading log finished more chapters,
but we cannot conclude that the log caused the difference because we did not assign
people to keep one and the result could reflect their existing motivation.

This correction illustrates a real boundary: a plausible explanation can still add a
fact that the source never supplied. The reader-flow gain cannot justify that addition.

## 4. Interface text: preserve a protected string

Source: The banner says “Read-only mode.” It appears when editing is disabled. This is
important. Users can still download a copy from the menu.

Revision: The banner says “Read-only mode” when editing is disabled, but users can still
download a copy from the menu.

Review: The sentence flows, but the proposed revision changed the protected string by
removing its period. That fails the literal-text constraint even though ordinary prose
punctuation might otherwise allow it.

Corrected final revision: When editing is disabled, the banner displays the exact text
“Read-only mode.” Users can still download a copy from the menu.

The short second sentence earns its place by stating a separate available action; the
protected string's punctuation takes priority over joining every clause.

## 5. Distinct admission: keep the pause

Source and retained revision: I forgot the attachment. The message referred to a budget
sheet, but I sent it before checking that the file had finished uploading.

Review: The first sentence has a distinct purpose. Joining it simply to make the average
sentence longer would weaken the admission without improving the explanation.

## 6. Empty commentary: delete it

Source: The reading room opens at 10 a.m. That is worth remembering. It closes at 6 p.m.,
and visitors need to return their requested materials fifteen minutes before closing.

Revision: The reading room opens at 10 a.m. and closes at 6 p.m., and visitors need to
return their requested materials fifteen minutes before closing.

Review: The empty reminder is gone, both opening hours remain, and the return deadline
is preserved. No new reason for the schedule was invented.

## Outcome and limits

The initial manual review caught two fidelity failures: an inferred group-formation fact
and altered punctuation inside a protected string. Both were repaired before acceptance.
Those failures led to explicit guards in the skill's review workflow. The six final
cases passed this assistant's manual fidelity and connection review; no blind reviewer,
commercial detector or statistical test of writing quality was used.
